# Community code of conduct

Replofy OS contributors, maintainers, and community members must keep project
spaces constructive and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Welcome contributors regardless of experience or background.
- Protect private information and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Accept maintainers' good-faith moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security advisory
flow. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban participants. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure about
reporters or affected people.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issue trackers, pull requests,
community spaces, and public situations where someone represents the project.
