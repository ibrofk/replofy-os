# Replofy Blog Skill

Use this skill when turning a Replofy workspace idea into a publishable blog
article. Keep the article grounded in workspace records and preserve a clear
review trail.

## Workflow

1. Read the current blog roadmap and identify the article's roadmap phase.
2. Gather the brief, source links, evidence cards, and related tasks before
   drafting.
3. Draft in small sections with a specific audience, thesis, and call to
   action. Mark unsupported claims for review instead of inventing evidence.
4. Run a review pass for factual support, structure, accessibility, and
   distribution metadata.
5. Save the article with its source registry ids and approval state, then
   record the intended distribution channels.

## Quality bar

- Every non-obvious claim has a source or an explicit review flag.
- The title, summary, status, roadmap phase, and distribution metadata agree.
- Drafts remain reviewable and reversible until an authorized user approves
  publication.
